# Phishing Incident Response Runbook

## 1. Purpose

This runbook provides guidance for identifying, investigating, and responding to suspected phishing incidents at Kente Logistics.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to employees, IT support personnel, and the security team responsible for handling suspicious emails and phishing-related incidents.

## 3. Initial Identification

A phishing incident may be suspected when an employee reports:

- An unexpected email requesting sensitive information.
- Suspicious links or attachments.
- Messages impersonating company management or trusted organisations.
- Requests for passwords, financial information, or account verification.
- Unusual login prompts after clicking an email link.

## 4. Initial Response

When a suspicious email is reported:

1. Record the date and time the email was reported.
2. Identify the recipient and sender.
3. Examine the email subject, links, attachments, and headers.
4. Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
5. Preserve the suspicious email for investigation.

Initial containment: Immediately quarantine the reported email and block its sender at the email gateway.

## 5. Investigation

The IT or security team should:

- Examine the sender's email address and domain.
- Inspect links and attachments in a controlled environment.
- Determine whether the employee clicked a link or opened an attachment.
- Check for suspicious account activity.
- Identify other employees who may have received the same message.

## 6. Containment and Remediation

Depending on the findings:

1. Remove or quarantine the phishing email from affected inboxes.
2. Reset compromised account credentials.
3. Revoke active sessions where necessary.
4. Block confirmed malicious domains and indicators.
5. Scan affected systems for suspicious activity.
6. Escalate confirmed compromises to the appropriate security personnel.

## 7. Recovery

- Restore affected accounts and systems to a secure state.
- Confirm that compromised credentials have been changed.
- Monitor affected accounts for unusual activity.
- Verify that malicious email messages have been contained.

## 8. Documentation

Record the following information:

- Incident reference number.
- Date and time of discovery.
- Affected users and systems.
- Sender address and suspicious URLs.
- Investigation findings.
- Containment and remediation actions.
- Final incident status.

## 9. Lessons Learned

After resolving the incident:

- Document how the phishing attempt was identified.
- Identify weaknesses in existing security controls.
- Recommend improvements to email security.
- Share relevant awareness guidance with employees.
